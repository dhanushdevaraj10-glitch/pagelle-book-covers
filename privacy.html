Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 • Version 1.0

Welcome to Pagelle Book Covers ("Pagelle", "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and web archive available at pagelle-book-covers.vercel.app (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide a rich, community-driven editorial book jacket gallery:

  • Account Information: When you register or sign in, we collect your name, email address, username, profile bio, and profile picture avatar.
  • User-Generated Content: When you design or upload book covers through Cover Studio or the cover submitter, we store the title, author, genre, description, designer credit, and cover image data you submit.
  • Interaction Data: Books you save to your favorites, accounts you follow, and follower lists to power your personal library and social community feed.
  • Technical Information: Standard request logs including browser type, IP address, and timestamp for security, abuse prevention, and rate-limiting.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Operate and display the book cover archive, member galleries, and curated collections.
  • Authenticate your account and maintain active sessions across devices.
  • Enable following other creators and saving book covers to your personal collection.
  • Prevent spam, unauthorized account access, and ensure service reliability.

3. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data to advertisers or third parties. We only share information in the following limited situations:

  • Public Community Profile: Book covers you publish and your public display name/bio are visible to other members of the community archive.
  • Service Providers: Secure cloud hosting infrastructure (e.g. Vercel) necessary to run and deliver the service.
  • Legal Compliance: When required by applicable law, regulation, or legal process.

4. User Rights & Account Deletion

You have full ownership and control over your account and data:

  • In-App Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your account at any time directly in the app by visiting your Profile → Edit Profile → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your profile and removes all covers and favorites associated with your account.
  • Email Request: You can also request complete account and data removal by contacting us directly at dhanushdevaraj10@gmail.com.

5. Security of Your Information

We use industry-standard encryption (TLS/HTTPS), salted password hashing, HMAC-SHA256 authenticated session tokens, and strict access controls to safeguard your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Service is intended for general audiences and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revisions will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
