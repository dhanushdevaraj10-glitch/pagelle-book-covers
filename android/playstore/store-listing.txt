App name (30 chars max):
Pagelle: Book Covers & Gallery

Short description (80 chars max):
Curate, explore, and design stunning book covers in a community archive.

Full description (4000 chars max):
Pagelle Book Covers is a curated visual archive and creative studio dedicated to editorial jacket art, contemporary book cover design, and community curation.

Whether you are a bibliophile, designer, typographer, or passionate reader, Pagelle brings the physical bookshelf aesthetic to your Android device with tactile elegance.

KEY FEATURES:

• CURATED EDITORIAL ARCHIVE
Explore iconic book covers spanning classic literature, modern fiction, speculative sci-fi, noir suspense, and modernist typography editions. Discover jackets created by world-renowned publishers, independent studios, and passionate book artists.

• LIVE COVER STUDIO
Unleash your creativity with the built-in Cover Studio. Generate custom book jacket designs with rich editorial layouts, Swiss-style typography, curated atmospheric color palettes, and bespoke geometric silhouettes in seconds.

• COMMUNITY SUBMISSIONS & DISCOVERY
Contribute your own book cover editions to the archive. Upload your illustrations, vintage jacket photos, or digital art. Follow other creators, view their portfolios, and celebrate book design together.

• PERSONAL FAVORITES & COLLECTIONS
Bookmark covers you adore into your personal library. Organize covers by genre—from "Sci-Fi, Speculative & Cosmic Visions" to "Modern Fiction & Contemporary Bestsellers."

• REAL-TIME SYNC & INSTANT UPDATES
Enjoy seamless synchronization across all your devices. Newly uploaded covers and profile updates appear in real time throughout the community gallery.

• DESIGNED FOR IMMERSIVE READING
Crafted with a luxury dark aesthetic, smooth gesture interactions, and fast responsive navigation inspired by the finest print publications.

Connect with our community of book lovers and designers worldwide. Submit your covers, curate your shelf, and celebrate the art of the book with Pagelle.
